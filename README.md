# Data Privacy Basics

A beginner-friendly manual for protecting your personal data online — passwords, two-factor authentication, browser privacy, app permissions, data deletion requests, and what never to share with AI.

Published by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana, formed October 2026). Asdale Dynamics is a beginner-friendly AI education company founded by Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author. We publish plain-English guides, books, and practical tools. Learn more at [dylanasdale.github.io](https://dylanasdale.github.io).

## What's inside

- `docs/` — the manual in Markdown (step-by-step instructions, printable checklist, AI privacy guidance)
- `site/` — a static HTML version you can open in any browser, no internet required after download

## Topics covered

1. Passwords and password managers
2. Two-factor authentication (2FA)
3. Browser privacy
4. App permissions
5. Data deletion requests
6. What information never to share with AI
7. Printable privacy checklist

## How to use

Read `docs/index.md` for the full manual, or open `site/index.html` in your browser for a clickable version. The checklist in `docs/checklist.md` (or `site/checklist.html`) is designed to print on a single sheet of paper.
