🚨 Emergency: If You're Breached

  1. Change password immediately on breached site + anywhere you reused it
  2. Enable 2FA if not already on
  3. Check haveibeenpwned.com for your email
  4. Freeze credit (US: Equifax, Experian, TransUnion) if SSN exposed
  5. Monitor accounts for suspicious activity
  6. Report identity theft at identitytheft.gov (US)
